
# Bob Chorba (Bulgarian Bean Soup)

## What do you need?

- [ ] 300g dried white beans (soaked overnight), or 2 cans of white beans, drained
- [ ] 2 tablespoons oil
- [ ] 1 large onion, finely chopped
- [ ] 2 carrots, diced
- [ ] 1 red bell pepper, diced
- [ ] 2 cloves garlic, minced
- [ ] 1 tablespoon paprika
- [ ] 2 tomatoes, grated, or 400g canned chopped tomatoes
- [ ] 1 potato, cubed (optional)
- [ ] 1 liter vegetable or chicken stock (plus more water as needed)
- [ ] a small bunch fresh mint or dried spearmint (mint is the classic bob chorba flavor)
- [ ] salt and black pepper
- [ ] a pinch of chili flakes (optional, for heat)
- [ ] fresh parsley, chopped, to serve

## How to make it

1. If using dried beans: drain the soaked beans, cover with fresh water, bring to a boil, then simmer for about 45–60 minutes until just tender. Drain and set aside. (Skip this step if using canned beans.)
2. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and carrots, and cook for 5 minutes until softened.
3. Add the bell pepper and garlic, and cook for another 2–3 minutes.
4. Stir in the paprika and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant — don't let it burn.
5. Add the grated tomatoes and cook for a few minutes until slightly thickened.
6. Add the cooked beans, potato (if using), and stock. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer.
7. Simmer for 20–25 minutes, until the potato is tender and the flavors have come together. Add more water or stock if it gets too thick.
8. Stir in the fresh or dried mint during the last 5 minutes of cooking.
9. Season with salt, black pepper, and chili flakes to taste.
10. Serve hot, topped with fresh parsley — a squeeze of lemon on the side is a nice touch too.
